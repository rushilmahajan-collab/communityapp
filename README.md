# Community App

Close the app. Open your world.

A countercultural app that brings people back to genuine human connection. No feeds, no likes, no engagement tricks — just real people meeting in real places.

## Project Structure

```
communityapp/
├── ios/                    # SwiftUI iOS app
│   └── CommunityApp/
└── backend/                # Node.js API (Railway)
    ├── src/
    ├── package.json
    ├── Dockerfile
    └── railway.toml
```

## The Mission

This app exists to bring people back to being human. The world is too digital, too AI-driven, too screen-based. People have lost genuine human connection.

**Every design decision must answer this question: Does this get someone closer to a genuine human connection, or does it keep them on their screen?**

## Values

- People over profit, always
- Safety and trust are non-negotiable
- No feeds, no likes, no social media mechanics
- The app is a tool, not a destination
- Christian values (love, generosity, accountability) guide the culture
- Negativity gets removed fast
- Impact over revenue

## Tech Stack

- **iOS**: SwiftUI (iOS 17+), MapKit, AVFoundation
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL
- **Hosting**: Railway (backend), App Store (iOS)
- **Storage**: AWS S3 for photos and video verification

See the build prompt for complete specifications.
